Delete Your KP Astro AI Account and Data

This page explains how users of KP Astro AI can request deletion of their account and associated app data.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email from the email address associated with your KP Astro AI account to:

mail2ashishkrsingh@gmail.com

Use the subject: KP Astro AI Account Deletion Request

Please include only the information necessary to identify your KP Astro AI account. Do not send your password, OTP, payment-card information, or other sensitive credentials.

Verification of the Request

To protect users from unauthorised deletion, we may need to verify that the request comes from the owner of the account before processing it.

Data That Will Be Deleted

After a valid deletion request is verified, we will delete the KP Astro AI account and associated account-linked data that is no longer required to be retained. Depending on the features used, this may include:

  • user profile information;
  • saved astrology charts;
  • saved prediction history;
  • saved Prashna history;
  • app preferences and language settings;
  • other account-linked app data stored for KP Astro AI functionality.

Information That May Be Retained

Certain limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary for:

  • security and fraud prevention;
  • legal or regulatory obligations;
  • accounting or transaction records where retention is required;
  • dispute resolution or enforcement of applicable terms.

Any retained information will only be kept for the period required for those purposes.

Google Play Purchases and Subscriptions

Deleting your KP Astro AI account does not automatically cancel subscriptions or other purchase obligations managed through Google Play.

Where applicable, subscriptions and purchases should also be managed through your Google Play account.

Processing Time

We aim to process valid account deletion requests within a reasonable period after identity and account ownership have been verified.
